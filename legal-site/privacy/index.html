Privacy Policy

Amplio Care is committed to protecting your privacy and the security of your personal health data. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

Last updated: 2026-08-03 Version: 1.0.1

Data we collect

We collect the following data:

  • Account info: email, name (for authentication)
  • Sensor data: movement data during tremor tests
  • Test results: frequency, amplitude, severity

We follow data minimization principles.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely for:

  • Providing rehabilitation training features and progress tracking
  • Showing in-app summaries and reports for your personal reference
  • Improving the app only if we use anonymized data with your consent

Data storage & security

When you sign in, account and synced training data may be stored with Google Firebase. Guest and local-only data stay on your device.

  • HTTPS/TLS for network traffic to our cloud services
  • Access limited by your account and Firestore security rules
  • You can export or delete data from the app

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal health data.

Data leaves the app only when:

  • You explicitly export or share files yourself
  • The law requires disclosure

The app does not currently send data directly to healthcare providers.

Your data rights

  • Access: view all your data
  • Portability: export your data
  • Erasure: delete your data
  • Withdraw: revoke consent anytime
  • Contact: for any privacy inquiries

Security measures

We protect your data with the security controls available in our current stack:

  • TLS for cloud requests
  • Firebase Authentication for signed-in accounts
  • Firestore rules that restrict access to your own data
  • On-device AES encryption for sensitive local health fields (such as tremor sensor samples and medication nicknames), with the key stored in the iOS Keychain
  • In-app export and account deletion

Files you export are written in a readable format for your own use. Full-file encryption of the entire local database is not claimed.

Optional medication nickname list

If you enable the optional medication nickname list, nicknames, reminder times, and check-ins are stored only on this device. Optional local notifications may fire on this device only if you allow notification permission; they are not sent to our servers. You can delete this data in the app. This feature is not medical advice and is not included in data export by default.

法律信息

隐私政策

Amplio Care 致力于保护您的隐私和个人健康数据的安全。本政策说明我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的信息。

最后更新：2026-08-03 版本：1.0.1

我们收集的数据

我们收集以下数据：

  • 账户信息：邮箱、姓名（用于身份验证）
  • 传感器数据：震颤测试期间的运动数据
  • 测试结果：频率、振幅和严重程度评估

我们遵循数据最小化原则，只收集必要的数据。

数据使用方式

您的数据仅用于以下目的：

  • 提供康复训练功能与进度追踪
  • 在 App 内向您展示摘要与报告（仅供个人参考）
  • 仅在使用匿名数据且获得您同意时用于改进应用

数据存储与安全

登录后，账号与已同步的训练数据可能存储在 Google Firebase；游客与仅本地数据保留在您的设备上。

  • 与云服务通信使用 HTTPS/TLS
  • 访问受账号身份与 Firestore 安全规则限制
  • 可在 App 内导出或删除数据

数据共享

我们不出售您的个人健康数据。

数据仅在以下情况离开本应用：

  • 您主动导出或分享文件
  • 法律要求披露

本应用目前不会直接向医疗机构发送数据。

您的数据权利

  • 访问权：查看您的所有数据
  • 可携带权：导出您的数据
  • 删除权：删除您的数据
  • 撤回权：随时撤回同意
  • 联系我们：如有隐私疑问请联系我们

安全措施

我们使用当前技术栈中可用的安全能力保护您的数据：

  • 云请求使用 TLS
  • 登录账号使用 Firebase Authentication
  • Firestore 规则限制仅访问您自己的数据
  • 对本机敏感健康字段（如震颤传感器采样、用药昵称）使用 AES 加密，密钥保存在 iOS Keychain
  • App 内提供导出与账户删除

您主动导出的文件为便于阅读的明文格式。本政策不宣称对整个本地数据库文件做全库加密。

可选的用药昵称清单

若您开启可选的用药昵称清单，昵称、提醒时间与打卡记录仅保存在本设备。若您允许通知权限，可选的本地通知仅在本机触发，不会上传至我们的服务器。您可在应用内删除这些数据。该功能不是医疗建议，且默认不包含在数据导出中。
